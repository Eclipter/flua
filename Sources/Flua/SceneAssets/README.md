# Wallpaper artwork

These original images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. They are bundled locally; playback never contacts an image service. Each background plate is 1586 × 992 pixels; the transparent jellyfish and lantern sprites are 1024 × 1536 pixels. The existing Ink Mountains pine atlas is 2128 × 739 pixels. The scenes animate mist and birds over Ink Mountains, separate pine sprites over its fixed artwork, windblown sand and dust over Sahara, streamlets along Contour Atlas waterways, flowing ink pigment over Optical Current, rising jellyfish over Pelagic, rising paper lanterns over Lantern Festival, and layered rain and ground splashes over After Hours. The painted pines in Ink Mountains remain part of the fixed background.

Ink Mountains and Sahara advance at a 2× base rate; Contour Atlas and Optical Current use the original 1× rate. The Speed control multiplies each scene's motion rate.
